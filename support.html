Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile → Delete account, and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, stalls, reviews and favourites and can't be undone. You can also email haiphamcr@gmail.com to request deletion.

A stall's details look wrong — what do I do?

Open the stall in the app and use the "Report this stall" button at the bottom of its page. Our team reviews reports and updates or removes incorrect listings.

How does "what's in stock today" work?

Any signed-in user who visits a stall can post a quick update about what they saw there. Updates automatically expire after a short period, so the feed stays current.

I'm a grower — how do I list my stall?

Create an account, choose "seller" (or add selling from your Profile with "Become a seller"), then use the + button to add your stall. New listings are reviewed before they go live on the map.

Why do you need my location?

Location is only used to show stalls near you and to plan routes — it's never used in the background. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

For anything else, including account or data requests, email haiphamcr@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
