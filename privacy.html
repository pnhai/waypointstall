Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Waypointstalls ("we", "us") operates the Waypointstalls mobile app and the Waypointstalls website (together, the "Service"), which helps people find roadside honesty box and farm gate stalls across Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have — written to comply with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information we collect

When you use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register, and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Location data— with your permission, your device's location is used to show stalls near you and to plan routes in Trip Planner. This is "when in use" only — we do not track your location in the background, and location is never stored against your account unless you explicitly turn on "Notify me about new stalls nearby", in which case we store a single approximate point and radius you choose.
  • Photos— if you list or manage a stall, photos you choose to upload are stored with our image host (Cloudinary) and shown publicly on that stall's listing.
  • Content you post— reviews, star ratings, stock updates ("what's in stock today"), stall listings, and reports of incorrect stall information.
  • Push notification token — a device token used to send you notifications (e.g. new stalls near a saved area), via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Basic usage data — such as how many times a stall listing has been viewed, to give stall owners simple visibility into interest in their stall.

We do not collect payment information — the Service does not process any payments. We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To operate core features: showing stalls on a map, planning routes, and letting sellers manage listings.
  • To let buyers and sellers interact — reviews, stock updates, favourites, and reports.
  • To send notifications you've asked for (new stalls nearby, account-related emails).
  • To keep the Service safe — investigating reports of incorrect or inappropriate content.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the Service, each only processing the data needed to provide their part of it: Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudinary (photo hosting), Firebase (push notifications and account verification email delivery), and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (address lookup — receives only the address text you search for, not your account identity). We do not share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Your name is shown next to reviews and stock updates you post, and stall listings show the stall (not the owner's personal details) publicly. Your email address is never shown publicly.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, your stall listings, reviews, favourites and stock updates. This cannot be undone. You can also request deletion by emailing haiphamcr@gmail.com.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or request a copy of the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us. Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe we have mishandled your information.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
